DeepChat did not begin as a full product. It started as an April Fools' joke.

The joke spread quickly on Xiaohongshu, and that made me wonder whether it could become a small product where everyone could join in and write their own versions of the joke. So I spent less than two days building DeepChat.

## Breaking: WeChat Will Introduce Deep Thinking

<a href="http://xhslink.com/o/9JuUKmzrwCK" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">View original post</a>

"People are already used to sending text messages in WeChat, but compared with more immediate forms of communication such as face-to-face conversation or video calls, asynchronous communication loses a sense of authenticity. Users have time to think, revise, and polish, and by the time the message is sent, the text no longer reflects what they really meant. Everyone is communicating through masks. Not only is this inefficient, it also creates misunderstanding and disagreement. More importantly, people are beginning to lose trust in one another.

If this continues for long enough, human civilization may face an existential crisis. Anyone who has read *The Three-Body Problem* knows that the Trisolarans have transparent thoughts. They do not disguise themselves, and their communication is far more efficient than ours. That is one reason Trisolaran civilization vastly surpasses Earth civilization. Of course, Trisolarans exist only in fiction and probably pose no real threat. But today we have already encountered an unfamiliar form of 'alien intelligence': AI. Anyone who has used DeepSeek knows that AI thoughts are also transparent. We can see their deep thinking. Isn't that a lot like the Trisolarans? If humanity fails to keep progressing, AI will become the Trisolarans of the real world, and human civilization will fall under AI control.

Therefore, the WeChat team has decided to learn from both DeepSeek and the Trisolarans by introducing a deep-thinking feature to make thought transparent. From now on, every text message sent in WeChat will automatically include the sender's deep thinking, so that everyone can see what they are truly thinking. This is a major practice in our effort to use technology for good. We believe that authentic communication can help human civilization develop faster and survive longer in the universe."

— WeChat Team, 04/01

## DeepChat

If every WeChat message came with visible deep thinking attached, what would human-to-human communication become?

It started as an April Fools' idea. I made a mockup in Figma and did not expect so many people to see it.

After the first post, I noticed that many people in the comments were writing their own genuinely funny variations. That made me think I could quickly build a website with AI where people could generate these "deep thinking" jokes together, and maybe the crowd would produce even better ones.

I combined the names WeChat and DeepSeek and called it DeepChat.

"From now on, no more WeChat, only Deep Belief."
