At the time, Cursor was getting a lot of attention, so I wanted to try making a small product with it.

This website lets a user enter a name, and then AI judges what the difference is between that person and a dog.

In the prompt, I explicitly told the AI to conclude that everyone is basically no different from a dog, but to invent the reasoning based on the person's name. That gave the whole thing a funny effect.
