This is a project I worked on at Reflex AI.

Existence is a product built around AI characters. Users can create their own characters, discover characters created by other people, and chat with them directly.

What made it different from similar products was mainly this:

1. You could pin a character you liked onto any webpage, so no matter what site you were browsing, you could bring that character back and continue the conversation. The page itself could become part of the topic.
2. We used a collaborative character-building system with GitHub-like ideas. Different users could improve the same character together, and when they disagreed on a direction, they could fork it. A single character could also have different versions for different periods or settings.

![Existence browser extension shown inside a webpage](/images/apps/existence/extension.png)

On the web app, you could create or browse characters, enter chats, and co-create with other users.
