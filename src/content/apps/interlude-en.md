A tiny extension that gives you something to do while AI is thinking.

Waiting for AI is a bit like waiting for an elevator. Sometimes it takes a while, sometimes only a moment, but either way it is boring.

So I made Interlude to make that waiting less dull.

After installing it, whenever you use ChatGPT or DeepSeek and the extension detects that the AI is thinking or generating an image, a small vocabulary card pops up automatically in the bottom-right corner. You can use that short pause to learn or review an English word, and once the AI finishes, the card disappears on its own.

I do not know how many words it will help you learn, but it definitely makes the wait less boring.

(You can also click the extension icon manually on any website to bring up the same small card.)
