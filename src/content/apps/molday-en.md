When it comes to us and our phones, who is taming whom?
You tell yourself you are done scrolling, but your finger keeps moving.
The pleasure feels like sugar:
sweet for a second, then only sticky emptiness remains.
And in the digital world, you just keep growing mold.

With Molday., we do not tell you how to live.
We simply look quietly at how you and your phone are doing today.
We do not say, "You need to change."
We only say, "This is where you are right now."

Sometimes admitting it takes more courage than changing it.

And if you want to mold a little less
and spend a little more time in real life,
you can "vacuum-pack" yourself once in a while
and protect the moldy version of yourself.
Want self-protection? Or want a friend to help protect you?
Molday. supports both.

To see it is to loosen its hold.
To admit it is to begin.

Give it a try.
