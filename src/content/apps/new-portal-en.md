I kept hearing people talk about building new "entry point" products, so I made one too, just to try it out.

New Portal is a small Chrome extension that looks at what you type and automatically decides whether you should use Google or ChatGPT.

In my daily workflow, both are indispensable. When the question is broad or time-sensitive, I use Google. When it is more specific or more personalized, I use ChatGPT.

So why not unify both behind a single input box? Every time you open a new browser tab, you can type whatever you want, and New Portal sends you to the right destination.

![Keyword mode: input is routed to Google first and related suggestions are shown](/images/apps/new-portal/store-1.png)

![Question mode: clearly shows that the query will be sent to ChatGPT, and Tab can be used to switch modes](/images/apps/new-portal/store-2.png)

The second screen makes it easy to browse your saved bookmarks. Once you have that, you can even hide the browser's built-in bookmarks bar and give more space back to the webpage itself.

![Second screen: a flatter, more portal-like layout for frequently used bookmarks](/images/apps/new-portal/store-3.png)
