OneSnap! is an iOS app built around collaborative photography with two devices. One device handles the actual shooting, while the other shows the live camera preview and can trigger the shutter remotely at the right moment.

It is meant to solve a very specific photography problem: bad framing, bad angles, and the awkward back-and-forth where the photographer and the subject can only communicate verbally. OneSnap! moves the step of "see first, then decide how to shoot" earlier in the process, so that two people can adjust the same frame together in real time.

Technically, it uses local networking or Bluetooth-like nearby connectivity to connect two phones directly. The connection is peer-to-peer and privacy-friendly: the preview stream goes straight between devices, without passing through a remote server or being uploaded anywhere.
