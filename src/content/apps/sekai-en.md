This is another project I worked on at Reflex AI. You can still find more information on the App Store, though the product is no longer operating.

In the past, interaction between people on social apps was relatively limited. sekai tried to use AI to open up new possibilities for that interaction.

Inside sekai, you could create AI characters and interact with your own characters directly.

At the same time, you could also chat with characters created by your friends, and your friends could see the chat history between you and those characters.

You could even play the role of a character yourself. When you sent a message, the LLM behind the scenes would transform it into the tone and style of that character.

Or you could have your own character proactively flirt with your friends.

Beyond simply using AI characters to connect people, we also designed many playful interaction patterns to make communication between friends richer and more interesting.
