A browser extension for watching YouTube at variable speed.

What makes it different from similar extensions is the interaction design: just long-press on the video and move up or down to adjust playback speed.

You can go all the way up to 10x speed and compress a one-hour video into about six minutes, which makes it much easier to judge whether the video is worth watching carefully. You can also skip through the boring parts quickly and slow back down for the parts that matter.

Compared with an AI summary, which may miss visual details you actually care about, speed control lets you keep the important parts of the video in view.
